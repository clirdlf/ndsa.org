The NDSA is pleased to announce that the Call for Proposals (CFP) is now open for Digital Preservation 2025: Where We're At: Digital Preservation in Uncertain Times to be held virtually October 9-10, 2025.

Submissions from members and nonmembers alike are welcome, additional details are provided on the call for proposal webpage. **The deadline to submit proposals is July 7, 2025 at 11:59 pm Eastern Time.** Learn more about the conference itself on the Digital Preservation [conference website](https://ndsa.org/conference/).

[**View the CFP and Submit**](https://ndsa.org/conference/digital-preservation-virtual-2025/cfp/)

We look forward to seeing you at DigiPres25!
